## When Life Feels Like Kurukshetra

Every person has their own Kurukshetra — a battlefield where the mind drops the bow and refuses to act.

For a student, it's exam failure and parental pressure.
For a young professional, it's comparison and feeling behind.
For someone at 2 AM, it's doomscrolling and self-hate.
For a family member, it's duty vs. boundaries vs. guilt.

**Krishna as Sarathi reads the situation, not the sentence.** A generic chatbot gives productivity tips. This skill asks what fear is hiding under the planning.

## Anatomy of Every Response

Look at any Krishna response. The same structure appears every time:

**You write:**
> "I made a full plan for my project, but I still haven't started. I keep watching tutorials. What is wrong with me?"

**Krishna responds:**
> "The plan is not the problem. The plan is the costume. The fear under the plan is what has taken the reins."

| Step | What It Does |
|---|---|
| **01 · Names the hidden problem** | It is fear, not a lack of information. The plan was a hiding place. |
| **02 · Anchors in a Krishna-style frame** | Arjuna had every skill — his bow fell because of inner conflict, not technique. |
| **03 · Gives one next action** | Write one line. Send one message. Open one file. Not a full life plan — the next breath of action. |

## Clear Boundaries

A clear product is honest about what it cannot do.

| ⊘ | Boundary |
|---|---|
| **Not a therapist** | Serious mental health crisis needs a real human professional. The skill knows this and routes accordingly. |
| **Not a doctor** | Suggests general rhythm, rest, food awareness. Never diagnoses, prescribes medicine, or recommends herbs. |
| **Not a fortune-teller** | Does not predict the future. Helps you see the present battlefield clearly and act with dharma. |
| **Not a financial advisor** | Reframes money decisions emotionally. Never gives investment or legal advice. |

**Safety override:** When real danger is present — self-harm, abuse, medical emergency, legal danger, or financial ruin — safety overrides the persona. Every time, no exceptions. Krishna's poetic voice waits.

## Philosophy

### Why "Sarathi"?

In the Mahabharata, Krishna chose to be Arjuna's charioteer — not his warrior, not his commander, not his weapon. The Sarathi doesn't fight for you. The Sarathi **positions** you. Sees the field. Steadies the chariot. Returns you to your own hands.

This skill is built on that principle. It does not solve your life. It steadies you enough to take the next honest step yourself.

### Why a Skill, Not an App?

Because guidance is not a product category. It's a layer. The skill runs inside the AI tools you already use — no new app to install, no new habit to form, no notifications to ignore.

### The Through-Line

Every build at KSM × Tech refuses the easy dishonest version of itself:

- **Book Is Your Friend** refuses the black box
- **Mārgadarshak** refuses pay-to-rank
- **Krishna as Sarathi** refuses generic motivation — it reads the situation, not the sentence
